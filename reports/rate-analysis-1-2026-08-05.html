6 · Rate Opportunities

1 · Reprice the dorm — largest single gain. +₹108/night (+22%) reaches Craft Hostels parity; +₹129 (+26%) reaches La GoYa. At either level it stays the cheapest or joint-cheapest rate in its band, and the ₹449 (+72%) whitespace above ₹620 means no competitor is encountered.
2 · Capture the dates the market is already lifting. On 5 nights the market median rises ≥4% above its own base while this room sits at or below its ADR: 2026-08-28, 2026-08-29, 2026-09-01, 2026-09-02, 2026-09-03.
3 · Apply the dorm's flex to every peak date, not one weekend. The dorm reaches +87% on its strongest night, yet sits below its own ADR on 8 of the 10 highest market-escalation dates. The capability exists; the calendar coverage does not.

7 · Rate Risks

1 · No floor beneath the dorm. At ₹491 nothing in the band is priced lower; the nearest competitor is ₹502, just +2% away. Any undercut leaves no room to respond and converts the rate into a loss-leading position.
2 · The room sits in a crowded band. 16 competitors within ±40% and 4 within ±6% means rate is not a defensible differentiator at the current level.
3 · Dorm rate movement reads as inconsistent. CV 0.23 (100th percentile of competitors) but concentrated on a single weekend rather than tracking market escalation dates — volatility without a visible calendar rule.
